# Join the Computer Science Club at Pitt (Pitt CSC)

HTML version: https://pittcsc.org/join/
Site summary: https://pittcsc.org/llms.txt

## Overview

The Computer Science Club at Pitt (Pitt CSC) is the largest student organization at the University of Pittsburgh and a partner of the School of Computing and Information. Open to all majors.

## How to Join

1. Join the email list: http://eepurl.com/bgY5c9
2. Add the Google Calendar: https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/render?cid=f64u131to44gn3tn8g62ov2u1s%40group.calendar.google.com
3. Keep in contact through the channels below.

## Meetings & Events

Meetings are typically held Mondays and Thursdays at 8pm. Events include hackathons, talks, and coffee chats.
