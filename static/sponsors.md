## Overview

The Computer Science Club at Pitt (Pitt CSC) is the largest student organization at the University of Pittsburgh and a partner of the School of Computing and Information. CSC gives partners direct access to a deep, engaged pipeline of computer science talent on campus and online.

## CSC at a Glance (2026-2027 Partnership Package)

- 1000+ active members
- Largest student organization at the University of Pittsburgh and partner of the School of Computing and Information
- 75+ events / year, including workshops, site visits, and socials
- 15K followers across social platforms
- 100M+ total views on reels
- 750K average views per video

## Why Sponsor?

- **Collaborative:** We work with sponsors to reach students and create a stronger community.
- **Connected:** Through our alumni network, we build connections between underclassmen, upperclassmen, and alumni.

## Annual Sponsorship Tiers

Every tier includes all benefits of the tiers below it. Benefits are valid for one year, from September 1 to August 31.

### Tier 1: Ada Lovelace — $1,000 / year
- Logo on the CSC website and Wiki
- Recruiting & campus outreach featured in weekly newsletters
- Ability to promote campus visits

### Tier 2: Alan Turing — $1,500 / year
Includes everything in Tier 1, plus:
- Logo on CSC at Pitt t-shirts
- Invitation to host one (1) custom company event

### Tier 3: John von Neumann — $2,500 / year
Includes everything in Tier 2, plus:
- Host up to two (2) custom company events
- Exclusive access to the club's member resume book

### Tier 4: Grace Hopper — $5,000 / year
Includes everything in Tier 3, plus:
- Host up to three (3) custom company events
- Exclusively sponsor a CSC initiative (Zero to Offer, Foundry, mentorship, etc.)
- Present at an event during Zero to Offer or Foundry
