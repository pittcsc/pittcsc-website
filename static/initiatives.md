## Overview

The Computer Science Club at Pitt (Pitt CSC) is the largest student organization at the University of Pittsburgh and a partner of the School of Computing and Information. Open to all majors. Pitt CSC runs career, project team, hackathon, mentorship, guest speaker, and social programs.

## Career

- **[Zero to Offer](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/z2o/)**: Student-run fall workshop series covering resumes, recruiting, and interviews for your first tech internship.
- **[Foundry](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/foundry/)**: A smaller community for consistent builders: interview practice, project work, presentations, and peer accountability.
- **[Mock Interviews](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/mock-interviews/)**: Practice technical, behavioral, and company-specific interviews with experienced peers and get honest feedback.
- **[Industry Recruiting Events](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/industry-recruiting/)**: Meet engineers and recruiters on campus through company tech talks, info sessions, and coding competitions.
- **[Site Visits](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/site-visits/)**: Tour Pittsburgh tech offices like Google, Duolingo, and Aurora and talk directly with local engineers.
- **[Launchpad](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/launchpad/)**: Hands-on software engineering workshops covering dev setup, data, UIs, and deployment to get you ready to build.

## Project Teams

- **[Dev Lab](https://pittcs.wiki/guides/skills/csc-dev-lab)**: Pitch or join a semester-long project team and practice Git, PR reviews, testing, and deployments.
- **[Consulting](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/consulting/)**: Apply to work on a small semester-long engineering team building and delivering real software for a partner company.

## Hackathons

- **[CSC Hacks](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/csc-hacks/)**: Pitt CSC's 48-hour internal hackathon for teams of up to three, wrapping up with 5-minute live demos and prizes.
- **[SteelHacks](https://steelhacks.org)**: Pitt's free annual hackathon hosted by CSC and SCI. Spend a weekend on campus building, learning, and demoing.

## Mentorship

- **[Bit/Byte](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/bit-byte/)**: Get paired with a Pitt CS upperclassman for class advice, resume help, and a friendly face in the club.

## Guest Speakers

- **[Fireside Chats](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/fireside-chats/)**: Live on-campus Q&A nights where Pitt alumni, startup founders, and tech creators talk candidly with students.

## Social

- **[Social Events](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/social-events/)**: Jeopardy nights, game nights, outings, and the Tech Club Formal so you can actually meet people outside class.
- **[Social Media](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/social-media/)**: Follow @csc.at.pitt for event updates, intern takeovers, member spotlights, and CS reels, or help us make them.
