## Overview

The Computer Science Club at Pitt (Pitt CSC) is the largest student organization at the University of Pittsburgh and a partner of the School of Computing and Information. Open to all majors. Pitt CSC runs career, project team, hackathon, mentorship, guest speaker, and social programs.

## Career

- **[Zero to Offer](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/z2o/)**: Our premier internship training program helping students land top-tier tech offers through mentorship and practice.
- **[Foundry](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/foundry/)**: A committed community for growth through interview practice, presentations, project building, mentorship, and peer accountability.
- **[Mock Interviews](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/mock-interviews/)**: Practice technical and behavioral interviews with experienced peers and industry professionals.
- **[Industry Recruiting Events](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/industry-recruiting/)**: Exclusive recruiting sessions and networking opportunities with top tech companies and sponsors.
- **[Site Visits](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/site-visits/)**: Visit local tech companies and offices to see real-world engineering environments and culture.
- **[Launchpad](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/launchpad/)**: An incubator program to help you launch your own startup or big idea from scratch.

## Project Teams

- **[Dev Lab](https://pittcs.wiki/guides/skills/csc-dev-lab)**: A hands-on development lab for students to build and collaborate on projects.
- **[Consulting](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/consulting/)**: Gain real-world experience building software solutions for local non-profits and startups in our community.

## Hackathons

- **[CSC Hacks](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/csc-hacks/)**: Our internal hackathon event designed to help members learn and collaborate on innovative projects.
- **[SteelHacks](https://steelhacks.org)**: Pitt's annual hackathon, hosted by Pitt CSC in partnership with the School of Computing and Information.

## Mentorship

- **[Bit/Byte](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/bit-byte/)**: Peer mentorship program connecting upperclassmen with underclassmen for academic and career guidance.

## Guest Speakers

- **[Fireside Chats](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/fireside-chats/)**: Intimate Q&A sessions connecting you with industry leaders, successful alumni, and tech visionaries.

## Social

- **[Social Events](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/social-events/)**: Fun gatherings, game nights, and outings to build a tight-knit community and make lasting friendships.
- **[Social Media](https://pittcsc.org/initiatives/social-media/)**: Stay connected with the club through our active social media presence and community updates.
