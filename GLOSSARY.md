# CSC CRM

The CSC CRM manages community accounts, contacts, and email campaigns.

## Campaign language

**Contact**:
A person represented in CSC's email database, with one delivery email address. A contact may exist without a CRM account or be linked to an account with a matching verified address.

**Campaign**:
A CSC email communication sent to a selected audience, such as a newsletter or event promotion.

**Campaign audience**:
Contacts selected using tags, account roles, graduation year, major, or attendance at a selected event, with all/any matching and exclusions.

**Contact tag**:
A label used to organize contacts and select campaign audiences. Tags do not grant CRM permissions.

**CSC announcements subscription**:
A contact's enrollment in CSC campaigns. Creating a CRM account enrolls its Pitt sign-in address unless that contact previously unsubscribed.

**Global unsubscribe**:
A contact's choice to stop receiving all CSC campaigns. CSV imports and account creation preserve this choice; the recipient can explicitly resubscribe.

**Campaign analytics**:
Aggregate delivery, bounce, complaint, unsubscribe, and link-click reporting for a campaign.

**Saved audience**:
A reusable set of selection rules for campaign recipients. Its contacts are determined when a campaign starts sending; exclusions take precedence.

**Event block**:
An editable copy of CRM event details inserted into campaign content. Later changes to the source event do not change the inserted details.

**Delivery block**:
A restriction preventing campaigns from being sent to a contact because of a hard bounce or complaint. It is separate from the contact's subscription choice.

**Uncertain send**:
A campaign email whose acceptance by the delivery provider could not be confirmed or ruled out. It requires review before another attempt can risk sending a duplicate.

**Archived contact**:
A contact removed from future campaign delivery while their existing campaign history is retained.

**Test email**:
A clearly labeled campaign preview sent to a specified test recipient and excluded from campaign analytics. It honors existing unsubscribe and delivery blocks.

**Campaign draft**:
An unfinished campaign shared among CSC officers, which any active staff member can resume preparing. Saving a draft does not schedule or send it.
