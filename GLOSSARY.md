# Pitt CSC CRM

The CRM manages CSC accounts, events, and member participation.

## Attendance

**Academic year**:
The CSC attendance reporting period from August 1 through July 31 in Pittsburgh time. The scheduled start of an event determines its academic year.

**Check-in**:
A member’s explicit confirmation of attendance at a CSC event. Each member has at most one check-in per event.

**Reported member**:
An active, verified CSC member eligible to appear in the attendance report, including members with zero check-ins and members who also hold staff, Foundry, or alumni roles.

**Events attended**:
The number of distinct events in the selected reporting year for which a member has a recorded check-in.

**Last event attended**:
The checked-in event with the latest scheduled start in the selected reporting year.
