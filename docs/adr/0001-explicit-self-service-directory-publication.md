# Keep directory publication separate from CRM membership

Students and alumni share Pitt email sign-in, but creating a CSC account must not enroll someone in AlumniDB. Users explicitly publish their shared profile after meeting the agreed completeness and graduation eligibility rules, and may unpublish without deleting their CRM profile; this preserves self-service without mandatory officer approval or automatic directory enrollment.

A published profile grants no staff or alumni role, and only explicitly selected directory fields become visible. Self-reported graduation year must be the current calendar year or earlier, without a month field or officer-configured cutoff; becoming eligible does not publish automatically. Officer moderation is a later feature rather than a dependency of the initial integration; the shared-data decision is recorded in ADR 0002.
