# Pitt CSC Directory

AlumniDB lets the Pitt CSC community find alumni with experience to share. Students and alumni use the same Pitt email identity to browse; publication depends on graduation year and profile completeness.

## Language

**Pitt email**:
A person's regular `@pitt.edu` email address, including one retained after graduation.
_Avoid_: Alumni email as a separate kind of address

**Directory**:
The collection of voluntarily published profiles available to people who can access the CSC CRM. Publication eligibility depends on graduation information and profile completeness.

**Directory profile**:
A person's directory-visible information from their shared CSC profile. Private CSC account information is not part of this visible profile.
_Avoid_: Account when referring to a directory entry

**Shared profile**:
The single profile a person maintains for the CSC CRM and AlumniDB. Its directory-visible information appears in AlumniDB when the owner publishes.
_Avoid_: Separate alumni profile when referring to a second editable copy

**Profile owner**:
The person represented by a directory profile, who maintains their own information.
_Avoid_: Officer when referring to the person responsible for their own profile

**Publication**:
The owner's explicit choice to share their directory profile in AlumniDB. Having a CSC account alone is not publication.
_Avoid_: Account creation when referring to joining the directory

**Profile last updated**:
The date of the most recent change to a person's directory information.
_Avoid_: Last login, last active

**Contact email**:
A non-Pitt email address a person shares for directory readers to contact them. It is separate from their Pitt sign-in address.
_Avoid_: Login email, recovery email

**Unpublishing**:
The owner's choice to remove their information from the directory while keeping their shared CSC profile.
_Avoid_: Account deletion, account ban

**Publication eligibility**:
Having a self-reported graduation year no later than the current calendar year and all required directory information, including at least one offered kind of help.
_Avoid_: CRM profile completion when referring to the directory's requirements

**Offered help**:
The ways a person is willing to help directory readers: referrals, mentorship, coffee chats, or resume review.
